# NNNN. Title in the imperative, for example "Use OpenAPI for published HTTP contracts"

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Rejected | Deprecated | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who made or approved the decision

## Context

What forces are at play: the problem, the constraints, and the facts that make a decision necessary now.

## Decision

What we decided, stated so that someone can act on it.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder. Which repositories must change, and how they migrate. What we will watch to know whether the decision was right.

## Alternatives considered

Each option that was seriously considered, and why it was not chosen.
