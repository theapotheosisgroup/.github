# Code of conduct

This code applies to everyone who takes part in The Apotheosis Group's repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces, and to anyone representing the organization in public.

## Our commitment

We want participation to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about work, not about people.
- Take responsibility for your mistakes, apologize to those affected and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use and build our software.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to an organization owner through GitHub, or to the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) once it is set. Reports are handled promptly and confidentially.

Organization owners may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code. They may warn, temporarily restrict or permanently ban a participant, depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is.
